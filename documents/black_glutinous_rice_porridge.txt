Black glutinous rice porridge is a popular Indonesian dessert made from black sticky rice that is slowly cooked with water until it becomes soft and creamy. It has a naturally sweet and slightly nutty flavor, while coconut milk is often added to provide a rich and creamy taste. This traditional dessert is commonly served warm or cold and is enjoyed as a simple snack or after-meal treat. Its distinctive dark purple color and chewy texture make black glutinous rice porridge both delicious and visually appealing.
