Eminem, born Marshall Bruce Mathers III, is an American rapper, songwriter, and record producer widely regarded as one of the most influential figures in hip-hop. He rose to international fame with albums such as The Slim Shady LP, The Marshall Mathers LP, and The Eminem Show, known for their complex wordplay, storytelling, and often provocative themes. His song “Lose Yourself,” from the film 8 Mile, became one of his most recognizable works and earned him an Academy Award for Best Original Song.
